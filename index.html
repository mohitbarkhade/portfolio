01 — About me

A little
about me

I'm part of the Validation team at Unbox Robotics, where I work on the systems that run our robotic parcel sortation — from induction and scanning to robots, bins and the software that connects them.

My job is to make sure every release is reliable. I validate new versions, write automated tests for real-world scenarios, and work closely with developers to find and fix issues early.

Before Unbox, I spent over three years at HCLTech — first as an intern on AS400 and SQL data validation, then as a QA Automation Engineer building Selenium, TestNG and Cucumber frameworks wired into Jenkins CI/CD. I bring that web-automation background into robotics testing every day.
